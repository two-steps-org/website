# Brag Plan: Two Steps AI

## What is this app?
Two Steps AI is a Tel Aviv studio that builds custom AI — chat agents, voice agents, CRMs and SaaS — and its new site is a 2026 monochrome design with a living network diagram at its heart.

## The angle
"Always Be *Ahead*" played as a quiet, confident product film. The site's own signature moves carry the video: light Inter Tight display type with one Instrument Serif italic word, the hub-and-six-nodes network assembling itself, the dark→light band flip, and real case-study numbers counting up. No hype words — the numbers and the diagram do the bragging.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black `#0a0a0c`. "Always Be" rises word by word in Inter Tight 300; "*Ahead*" lands in Instrument Serif italic a beat later. Then the second line: "AI Built Just For You".

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero network builds itself: Two Steps mark in a glass hub, six lines draw out, six node cards arrive one by one (LLM Engine, Vector Store, Realtime API, Security, Analytics, Connectors), light pulses travel the lines.
- The screen flips to the site's light band (`#fafafa`): "Automate Roles, Not Just Tasks" + the four things they build, arriving as cards.
- Back to dark: three real results count up — 80% of bookings automated, +20% conversion, −30% delivery times.

## Outro / punchline
Two Steps wordmark. "Always Be *Ahead*". A cursor presses the white "Book a Call" pill. `twosteps.ai`.

## User flow worth showing
none — landing-page only. Closest thing to a flow is the visitor's path: see the network → see what gets built → see results → press "Book a Call". The outro simulates that press; the "Talk to our AI now" launcher (mic badge, green online dot) sits bottom-right in the outro as a real UI detail.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: monochrome product film with visible choreography — restraint in palette, not in motion
- Interpretation: few scenes, long readable holds, but every entrance is a real move (word staggers, line draws, theme flip, count-ups); nothing timid, nothing flashy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0a0a0c` (light band: `#fafafa`, border `#e6e6e6`, text `#0a0a0a`)
- Accent: `#f5f6f8` (the accent *is* the ink; single colour accents: `#4ade80` online dot)
- Text: `#f5f6f8`, muted `#8a8f98`
- Surfaces: glass `rgba(20,22,27,0.55)` + border `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, radius 20px / 12px
- Display font: Inter Tight 300, tracking −0.021em; signature word in Instrument Serif italic
- Body font: Inter
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`
- Strongest visual element: the hero AI network diagram (hub + 6 nodes + travelling pulses)

## Share copy (draft)
New site is live. Custom AI agents, CRMs and SaaS — built just for you. Always be ahead. twosteps.ai

## Audio direction
- Role: steady, clean bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady and clean; ~110 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume 0.45 (the track is mastered quietly; 0.32 measured −27.5 dB mean), fade-out baked into the last 1.6s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (`assets/music/cues/…vol-12….music-cues.md`). Strong cues to lock: **8.74s** (flip to light band), **13.11s** (flip back to dark / results), **17.47s** (outro logo). Beat-grid windows: nodes 4.39–7.09 (one per beat — short labels, then the full set holds ≥1.5s); service cards 9.29–10.93 (one per beat, set holds ~2s); stats every other beat 13.64 / 14.73 / 15.84.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hub glow / ambient background gradient breathes with RMS. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse (3–4), soft, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: node arrivals (soft drops on first/last only), theme flip (soft impact), Book a Call click (mouse click), logo landing (soft bong)
- Restraint rule: no glitch, no punches, nothing louder than the bed by much; never score every node.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.3s (0.00–3.27)
Dark bg with soft ambient gradient. "Always Be *Ahead*" (line 1) then "AI Built Just For You" (line 2), word-stagger rise, large display type. Verbatim hero copy.
Sequential/interaction: words rise one by one (0.035–0.06s stagger), full headline settled ≥1.5s.
Audio intent: bed enters, calm confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the music open).
Transition mood: soft — headline slides left/shrinks to make room → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The network — 5.5s (3.27–8.74)
Recreate the hero: left, body line "You bring the vision, we bring it to life." with the four glass chips; right, the network diagram. Hub pops in with the Two Steps mark, six lines draw from hub outward, six node cards arrive one by one clockwise from top (title + uppercase desc exactly as on site), then pulses travel the lines while the set holds.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 6 node cards on beats 4.39→7.09, set holds to 8.74.
Audio intent: gentle build.
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on first and last node only.
Transition mood: dramatic-but-clean — theme flip wipe to light → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — What we build — 4.4s (8.74–13.11)
Light band `#fafafa`. Eyebrow "Services", headline "Automate Roles, Not Just Tasks". Four bordered cells in the ProofStrip-style grid: AI Chat Agents · AI Voice Agents · CRM Development · Custom SaaS — grid lines draw open from the left, labels rise into cells.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 labels on beats 9.29→10.93, full set holds ~2s.
Audio intent: brightest moment of the bed.
Audio-coupled idea: soft impact on the flip at 8.74 (beat-locked).
Transition mood: clean — flip back to dark → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Results — 4.4s (13.11–17.47)
Dark. Eyebrow "Case studies". Three glass stat cards with count-ups, using real case-study data:
- **80%** — of bookings automated · AI Chat Agent, Independent Barber
- **+20%** — conversion rate · AI SDR Call Agent, Leads Manager
- **−30%** — delivery times · Custom SaaS, Logistics Company
Sequential/interaction: yes — cards at 13.64 / 14.73 / 15.84 (every other beat), numbers count up over ~0.8s, set holds ≥1.2s.
Audio intent: payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: light chip/drop tick as each number lands.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.5s (17.47–21.00)
Dark. Two Steps wordmark lands at 17.47 (beat-locked). "Always Be *Ahead*" under it. White "Book a Call →" pill; a cursor glides in and presses it at ~18.56 (pill scales 0.97 then back). `twosteps.ai` in muted text. Bottom-right: the "Talk to our AI now" glass launcher with mic badge + green dot.
Sequential/interaction: simulated cursor click on Book a Call.
Audio intent: resolve, fade.
Audio-coupled idea: soft bong on logo, mouse click on press; music fades out 19.5→21.
Transition mood: hold, fade to black last 0.3s.

**Music mood for this video:** steady / clean / confident
**Audio summary:** A clean 110 BPM bed opens under the headline, the two theme flips and the logo land on strong cues, a handful of soft UI sounds mark arrivals and the final click, and the bed fades under the wordmark.
